# Wither Plus

Wither Plus reworks the vanilla Wither into a configurable three-stage boss encounter built around soul flames, bones and the black sun.

## Features

- Twelve skills, including soul-fire sweeps, skull meteors, soul chains, shadow dashes, bone spires, a black sun, a maelstrom, death marks, projectile barrages and expanding tides.
- Forced phase-transition attacks: Bone Crown and Requiem.
- Phase changes based on the Wither's remaining health percentage.
- Custom shaders, glowing textures, ground warnings, camera shake and screen effects.
- Configurable health, attack damage, knockback, phase thresholds, skill cooldowns, damage multipliers and selection weights.
- Client options for screen effects, the glow layer and camera shake.

Skill damage scales with the Wither's attack attribute. Knockback respects the target's knockback resistance.

## 中文说明

Wither Plus 重做原版凋零，以魂焰、骸骨和黑日为主题，带来可配置的三阶段首领战。

- 包含十二个技能，涵盖魂焰扫射、骷髅陨落、灵魂锁链、暗影冲刺、骨刺、黑日、漩涡、死亡标记、弹幕和扩散潮汐。
- 转阶段强制释放骸骨王冠与安魂曲。
- 使用剩余生命百分比判断阶段。
- 保留自定义着色器、发光贴图、地面预警、摄像机震动和屏幕效果。
- 可配置生命、攻击力、击退、阶段阈值、各技能冷却、伤害倍率和抽取权重。
- 客户端可以设置屏幕效果、发光层与摄像机震动。

技能伤害基于凋零攻击属性与技能倍率计算，击退受目标击退抗性影响。
